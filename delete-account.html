Delete your Mythwake account

You can ask us to delete your Mythwake account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. This page is for the online game Mythwake (playmythwake.com), on every platform it's on.

How to ask for deletion

By email

  1. Email baynav@playmythwake.com from the email address on your Mythwake account, with the subject Delete my Mythwake account.
  2. If you can, tell us your character's name. Don't send your password: we'll never ask for it.
  3. If the email doesn't come from your account's address, we'll reply to that address to check the request really comes from you.
  4. We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

From the game

In the game, Settings > Delete account opens this page. Then send the email above: that's the request, and we delete the account within 30 days.

Deleting can't be undone. Log out of the game before we delete the account.

What we delete

  • Your account: your email address, your password (stored only as a hash), saved login keys, and any linked Steam or other platform login.
  • Your character and everything it has: levels and skills, items, bank, coins, quests, friends and ignore lists, guild membership, market offers and mail.
  • Your place on the hiscores: it disappears at the next hiscores update (within about 10 minutes).

What we keep, and for how long

  • Chat and activity logs (chat, trades, market and moderation records) are kept for 90 days from when they were made, then deleted, as for every player. We keep them to investigate reports, cheating and abuse.
  • Crash and error reports are kept for 90 days, then deleted.
  • A short record that the account was deleted (the account name, the date and the request) is kept for 30 days for security, fraud prevention and legal reasons, then deleted.
  • Backups: the account is removed from the live game at once and from our backups as they roll over, within 30 days.

Questions? Email baynav@playmythwake.com. Our Privacy Policy explains everything we collect.
