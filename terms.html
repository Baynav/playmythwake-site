Terms of Service and Rules

Last updated: 12 November 2026

By creating an account or playing Mythwake you agree to these terms. Mythwake is run by Baynav ("we").

Your account

  • One person per account. Keep your password and email safe: you're responsible for what happens on your account.
  • You must be at least 13 to play (or the minimum age where you live, which is up to 16 in parts of the EU).
  • Don't share, sell, buy or trade accounts.
  • You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing baynav@playmythwake.com from the address on your account. It can't be undone.

Rules of play

  • No cheating: no bots, macros that play for you, hacked clients, exploiting bugs, or tools that read or change the game's memory or network traffic. Report bugs instead of using them.
  • No real-money trading: don't buy or sell gold, items or accounts for real money or anything outside the game.
  • Be decent in chat: no harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content directed at others, doxxing (sharing someone's private information) or spam. Player-versus-player fighting in the Wilderness is part of the game; harassing people outside it isn't.
  • No scams: trade fairly. Don't trick people out of items with fake trades or lies.
  • Names: character and guild names mustn't be offensive or impersonate staff.
  • Staff: don't pretend to be one of the team.

What we can do

  • Mute, temporarily ban or permanently ban accounts that break these rules, remove items or coins gained by breaking them, and reset characters where needed. We'll tell you why.
  • Change, balance, add or remove content, run a beta wipe (announced in advance) and change these terms (we'll say so in the game's news).
  • Mythwake is offered "as is": we work hard to keep it running, but can't promise it will always be available or free of bugs.

Your content

Chat and names you create stay yours, but you let us show, store and moderate them to run the game.
