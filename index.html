Welcome to Mythwake

Mythwake is an online 2D RPG set on one island that every player shares. Train your skills, fight with the weapon style you like, explore dungeons and take on the bosses, side by side with everyone else.

What is Mythwake?

  • One shared worldTowns, roads, rivers and wild lands on one island, with every player in it together.
  • 14 skillsFight, gather and craft: Melee, Archery, Sorcery, Prospecting, Logging, Angling, Forging, Alchemy and more.
  • BossesBig fights with moves of their own and rare drops worth the grind.
  • DungeonsEach with its own layout, its own monsters and a boss room at the end.
  • QuestsStories in every town, and a tutorial island to start you off.
  • Play togetherParties, guilds, friends, trading and the Grand Market.
